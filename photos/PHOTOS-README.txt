DAYLIGHT SNEAKERS — where the product photos go
===============================================

Drop sneaker photos into this folder. Naming tells me what each one is:

  brand_model_colourway_angle.jpg

Examples:
  nike_airmax95_freshmint_side.jpg
  nike_airmax95_freshmint_34front.jpg
  nike_shoxtl_black_side.jpg
  nike_shoxtl_black_rear.jpg
  nike_freemetcon6_racerblue_top.jpg

Angles I use, in order of importance:
  side     - the main catalogue tile. One pair, side profile, plain background.
  34front  - three-quarter front. Second image on the product page.
  rear     - heel-on.
  top      - top-down.
  sole     - outsole.
  detail   - close crops, nice for the product page but never the tile.

For the 360 spinner on the homepage, shoot one pair on a turntable and
name the frames:
  turntable_01.jpg ... turntable_36.jpg
Twenty-four frames is the minimum that looks smooth. Thirty-six is better.

What NOT to put in here:
  - campaign posters with text baked into the image
  - anything with a person in the frame (those belong in a "lifestyle" folder)
  - screenshots
